RedRound runs authorized pentests on websites you own. You register the domains and URLs to test, prove that you own them, group them into an application, and submit a pentest. Every finding in the report is reviewed by a security researcher before it is published, and comes with its location, the evidence, and the steps to fix it.

This documentation covers how each part of the product works: what you set up, what RedRound checks before testing starts, what the report contains, and how billing and data handling work.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Target">
    In **Assets**, add a domain or a URL on a domain. RedRound files it under an Asset automatically. See [Assets and Targets](/core/assets-and-targets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify ownership">
    Publish a verification value on the domain. Nothing is tested until ownership is verified. See [Ownership verification](/verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the Target to an Application">
    An Application is the product or system you want tested. Each pentest covers one Application. See [Applications](/core/applications).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a pentest">
    Choose the Targets, set the test window and exclusions, and confirm authorization. See [Test scope](/core/pentests/test-scope).
  </Step>
</Steps>
