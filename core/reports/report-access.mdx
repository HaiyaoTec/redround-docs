---
title: "Report access and unlocking"
description: "Summary, free full, and standard full report access; when a report needs unlocking; and how long each report is kept."
---

Who can read a report, and for how long, depends on how the pentest was paid for and what it found.

## Access levels

| Access | When it applies | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Summary** | The free pentest found at least one Medium, High, or Critical finding, and the report is not unlocked yet. | Five items: the number of reviewed findings, the severity distribution, the tested scope, the test dates, and the unlock price. |
| **Free full** | The free pentest found nothing at Medium or above. | The full web report and PDF. No Retest rounds are included. |
| **Standard full** | The pentest used a plan credit or a Single report purchase, or the report was unlocked. | The full web report and PDF, with two Retest rounds. |

While a report is at Summary access, finding titles, locations, evidence, and remediation are not sent to your browser, and they do not appear in the console home page, search, or email.

## Unlock a report

Only the workspace owner can unlock a report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report summary">
    Go to **Reports** and open the report, or use **Report ready to unlock** on the console home page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to unlock">
    If you have a paid credit available, RedRound asks you to confirm using one credit. Otherwise, buy a Single report for $999.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the full report">
    The full report is available immediately. Unlocking does not run a new pentest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can unlock a report until **27 days** after it was first published. The free pentest credit cannot be used to unlock a report.

## How long reports are kept

| Report | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Summary or free full report | 30 days after first publication |
| Standard full report | 12 months after first publication |

The retention period counts from first publication. Unlocking later or canceling your subscription does not change it. If a Retest is in progress when a report would expire, the report and evidence are kept until at least 30 days after the Retest result is published.

## Restricted access

Access to a report can be restricted during a payment dispute, a content security issue, or a deletion request. The report page shows the reason category. Evidence downloads and new Retest requests are unavailable while a restriction applies. Disputing a finding's severity does not restrict the report.

## Downloading the PDF

Download the PDF from the report page. Each PDF corresponds to one report version. Retest results have their own PDF on the **Retests** tab. RedRound does not email PDFs; email notifications link to the report page, which requires signing in.
