description: "Start a subscription, switch between monthly and yearly billing, and what happens to credits when you change."
---

The workspace owner manages the subscription from **Settings › Billing**.

## Start a subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Settings › Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then complete payment in Stripe Checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use your credits">
    Credits for the first service month are granted as soon as payment succeeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch between monthly and yearly

Open **Settings › Billing** and select **Manage subscription** to open the Stripe customer portal.

- A switch takes effect at the end of the current paid month or year.
- There is no prorated charge or credit for the remaining period.
- Credits already granted stay valid until their normal expiry.

## Service months

A service month starts on your subscription's start date each month. If that day does not exist in a month, such as the 31st, the service month starts on the last day of that month and returns to the original day afterward.

## Several workspaces

A subscription belongs to one workspace. Credits cannot be moved between workspaces.
