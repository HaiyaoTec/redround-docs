## Cancel a subscription

The owner cancels from **Settings › Billing** › **Manage subscription**. Cancellation stops future renewals.

After canceling:

- The current paid month or year continues until it ends. For yearly plans, the remaining monthly credits of the paid year are still granted.
- Reports already received keep their full access and 12-month retention.
- Retests already accepted continue.

## Refunds

- A purchased full report cannot be refunded.
- Used credits are not returned, including when you disagree with a finding's severity or expected different results.
- Unused credits from a past service month are not refunded or carried over.

Factual errors in a report are corrected by a new report version. Billing errors are corrected. See the [Refund Policy](https://redround.com/refund-policy) for the full terms.

## Canceling a pentest

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Canceled before testing starts, paid with a credit | The credit is released. |
| Canceled before testing starts, paid as a Single report | The payment is kept as one pentest for this order. Resubmit at any time after confirming scope and authorization. |
| Stopped after testing starts | The credit or payment is used. A partial report is delivered if available. |

## Platform problems

If a platform reason prevented RedRound from covering the agreed scope, you can request compensation instead of a refund. See [Compensation](/core/pentests/pentest-statuses#compensation).
