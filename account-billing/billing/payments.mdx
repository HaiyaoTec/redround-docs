---
title: "Payments"
description: "How RedRound takes payment through Stripe, which payment methods are accepted, and who in the workspace can pay."
---

RedRound takes payments through Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's pages and are not stored by RedRound.

## Who can pay

Only the workspace owner can buy a Single report, use a paid credit, or manage the subscription. When a member reaches a payment step, the page shows that the owner must complete it.

## Payment methods

Stripe Checkout shows the payment methods available for your country and currency. Prices are in US dollars.

## When you are charged

| Purchase | Charged |
| --- | --- |
| Single report for a new pentest | After the scope check passes and before testing starts. The pentest shows **Awaiting payment** until payment succeeds. |
| Single report to unlock a report | When you confirm the unlock. |
| Monthly subscription | At the start of each service month. |
| Yearly subscription | At the start of each service year. Credits are still granted monthly. |

## Failed renewal

If a subscription renewal fails, Stripe retries the payment for up to 14 days from the first failure. During this period:

- The console home page shows **Payment failed** to the owner.
- New credits for the service month are not granted until payment succeeds.
- Accepted pentests and existing reports are not affected.

Update the payment method from **Settings › Billing**. If payment still fails after 14 days, the subscription ends. Existing reports and accepted Retests remain available for their retention periods.

## Taxes

Taxes are calculated at checkout based on the billing details you provide. To show a legal name and tax ID on invoices, enter them at checkout.
