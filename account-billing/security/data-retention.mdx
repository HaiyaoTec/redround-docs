## Retention periods

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Summary and free full reports, with evidence | 30 days after first publication |
| Standard full reports, with evidence | 12 months after first publication |
| Reports with a Retest in progress | Until at least 30 days after the Retest result is published |
| Test parameters | With the pentest, deleted with the report content |
| Testing accounts | Until you remove them, or they are removed automatically after the pentest |
| Cookie consent records | 24 months |

When a report expires, its content, evidence, and PDFs are deleted. A record of the pentest remains without report content.

## Records kept after deletion

Some records are kept after you delete a report, a workspace, or your account, because they are needed for legal, billing, or authorization purposes:

- Authorization records: who authorized each pentest, when, and under which terms.
- Ownership verification records.
- Billing and payment records.
- Audit records.

## Deleting a workspace or account

Deleting a workspace or account removes access immediately and starts deletion of report content, evidence, and PDFs, including copies in backups. See [Workspaces](/core/workspaces#deleting-a-workspace) and [Account security](/account-billing/security/account-security#delete-your-account).

Copies of emails already delivered to your inbox cannot be deleted by RedRound.
