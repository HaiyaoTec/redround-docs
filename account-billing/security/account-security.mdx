Manage your personal account in **Profile**. These settings apply to you in every workspace you belong to.

## Sign-in methods

You can sign in with:

- **Email and password**
- **Google**

Keep at least one sign-in method. You cannot disconnect Google if you have not set a password.

### Connect Google

In **Profile › Authentication**, select **Connect** next to Google. The Google account's email must be verified and must match your RedRound email address. RedRound does not store your Google profile photo.

## Password

Set or change your password from **Profile › Authentication**. RedRound sends a link to your email address to confirm the change.

## Sessions

Select **Sign out other sessions** in **Profile › Authentication** to end every session except the current one. Sessions signed out this way end within 5 minutes. Signing out on the current device takes effect immediately.

## Change your email

In **Profile › General**, enter a new email address. RedRound sends a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect when you open it.

## Delete your account

In **Profile › General**, select **Delete account**. RedRound sends a confirmation link, valid for one hour.

Before you can delete your account:

- If you own a workspace with other members, transfer ownership first.
- If you are the only member of your workspace, deleting your account also deletes the workspace, which requires no active subscription and no pentest in progress.

Deletion removes your account, sessions, and memberships immediately. Authorization, order, and billing records that RedRound must keep are retained. See [Data retention](/account-billing/security/data-retention).
