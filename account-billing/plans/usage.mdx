---
title: "Credits and usage"
description: "How pentest credits are reserved, used, and released, and where to see your available credits."
---

**Settings › Usage** shows the credits available this service month, by source, with their expiry dates and any reserved credits. Owners and members can view it.

## Credit sources

| Source | Granted | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subscription** | 2 per service month after payment succeeds | End of the service month |
| **Free** | 1 per service month after activation | End of the service month |
| **Compensation** | After an approved compensation request | 30 days after approval |

## When a credit is reserved, used, or released

| Event | Effect on the credit |
| --- | --- |
| Saving or editing a draft | None |
| Pentest accepted | Reserved |
| Testing starts | Used |
| Canceled, rejected, or failed before testing starts | Released |
| Stopped after testing starts | Stays used |
| Report unlocked with a credit | Used when the unlock completes |

A released credit keeps its original expiry date. Canceling and resubmitting does not extend it.

## What does not use a credit

- Registering or verifying Targets
- Creating Applications and Test plans
- Requesting a Retest
- Reading a report that already has full access

One pentest uses one credit, however many Targets or Assets it includes. Unlocking a report that is already covered by a credit does not use a second one.

## Larger scopes

There is no limit on the number of Assets or Targets you can register. When one pentest includes Targets from more than 30 unique Assets, RedRound shows a prompt to contact us. Several Targets on the same Asset count as one, and Assets not in this pentest are not counted.
