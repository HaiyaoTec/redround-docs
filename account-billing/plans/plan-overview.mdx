All prices are in US dollars. Taxes are added where applicable. Plans are not priced by the number of Assets or Targets.

## Plans

| | Free pentest | Single report | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | $0 | $999 per report | $1,599 per month | $15,999 per year |
| **Pentests** | 1 per service month | 1 per purchase | 2 per service month | 2 per service month, 24 per year |
| **Report access** | Summary, or free full if nothing Medium or above | Standard full | Standard full | Standard full |
| **Retest rounds** | None | 2 per report | 2 per report | 2 per report |
| **Report kept for** | 30 days | 12 months | 12 months | 12 months |
| **Credit card required** | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |

A **pentest** here means one full pentest of one Application, or unlocking one existing report. Both use one credit.

## Every standard full report includes

- The full web report and PDF, reviewed by a security researcher.
- Two Retest rounds, requested within 30 days of receiving full access. See [Retests](/core/reports/retests).
- Retention for 12 months after first publication.

## Single report

Buy a Single report for $999 in two situations:

- **New pentest**: you pay after the scope check passes and before testing starts. The full report is included, whatever the findings.
- **Unlock an existing report**: you pay to unlock the full version of a free pentest's report. No new testing runs.

## Subscriptions

- Credits belong to the workspace. They are not tied to an Application, Asset, or Target.
- Each service month grants 2 credits. Unused credits do not carry over to the next month.
- Yearly plans grant credits month by month. You cannot use a future month's credits early.
- When you run out of credits, buy a Single report for $999. There are no discounted credit packs, automatic top-ups, or stacked subscriptions.

### Which plan costs less

| Your usage | Lower total cost |
| --- | --- |
| 1 pentest in a month | Single report: $999 |
| 2 pentests in a month | Monthly: $1,599 (saves $399 compared with two Single reports) |
| 1 pentest per quarter (4 per year) | Single reports: $3,996 |
| 1 pentest per month (12 per year) | Single reports: $11,988 |
| 2 pentests every month (24 per year) | Yearly: $15,999 (saves $7,977 compared with 24 Single reports) |
